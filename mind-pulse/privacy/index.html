Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Mind Pulse turns the notes you play on a keyboard into visuals. It does this entirely on your iPad.

What Mind Pulse does with sound

Mind Pulse uses the microphone to hear your keyboard. The sound is analysed on your iPad, in the moment, to work out which notes are being played. It is never recorded, never saved, and never sent anywhere.

If you turn the microphone off in Settings, Mind Pulse still works with a keyboard connected by cable, or by touching the screen.

What Mind Pulse does with a connected keyboard

When a keyboard is connected by cable, Mind Pulse reads which keys are pressed and how hard. This is used only to draw the visuals. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

What is stored

Mind Pulse remembers two settings on your iPad: the theme you chose, and whether crazy mode is on. Nothing else is stored.

What is collected

Nothing. Mind Pulse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Children

Mind Pulse collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
