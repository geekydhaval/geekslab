# geekslab

Landing page for geekslab — tech and AI explained in Gujarati, balanced between nature and the future (स्थितप्रज्ञ).

A small draggable world built with three.js (loaded from jsDelivr), hosted on GitHub Pages.

- `index.html` — page, brand, dock
- `main.js` — the world: nature on the left, the future on the right, Dhaval's head in the middle
- `models/head.glb` — Dhaval's head (Tripo multiview scan from a phone video, simplified to ~114k triangles)
- `models/hda_pod.glb`, `models/hda_class.glb` — furniture taken from the HDA office model, shown inside the glass tower
- `assets/` — app icons and the HDA H logo
- `mind-pulse/` — Mind Pulse app pages
